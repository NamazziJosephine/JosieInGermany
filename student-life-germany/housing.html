Finding a place to live

Three common options, and how to avoid the common traps.

🏢

Student residence (Wohnheim)

Rooms run by the student services organisation. Usually the most affordable, but there is often a waiting list, so apply early.

🛋️

Shared flat (WG)

A room in a flat with other people. Often you introduce yourself at a short "casting" with the current flatmates.

🔑

Your own flat

The most privacy, but usually the highest cost, and landlords often ask for several documents.

Quick comparison

Option Good for Watch out for
Student residenceLow cost, meeting peopleWaiting lists, apply early
Shared flatFlexibility, language practiceDifferent living habits, castings
Own flatPrivacyHigher cost, more paperwork

Beware of rental scams

  • Never pay a deposit or fees before you have seen the room or flat (or at least spoken to the landlord on video).
  • Be suspicious of offers that are far cheaper than similar places, or landlords who are "abroad" and only want to send keys by post.
  • A rental deposit (Kaution) is a normal practice, but it is limited by law. Ask your student services organisation if you are unsure.
