Arriving in Germany

The paperwork that comes first, in plain language.

In Germany you register the address where you live at the local registration office (Bürgeramt or Einwohnermeldeamt). This is normally due within about two weeks of moving in.

Usually needed:

  • Your passport or ID
  • The registration form, filled in
  • A confirmation from your landlord that you moved in (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung)

Students from outside the EU usually need a residence permit for study, which is handled by the immigration office (Ausländerbehörde). Citizens of EU countries generally do not need one, but still register their address.

Book an appointment early, since waiting times can be long. Ask your university's International Office which documents to bring.

Enrolment (Immatrikulation) makes you an official student. You typically hand in your admission letter, proof of health insurance and pay the semester contribution.

The semester contribution often includes a ticket for local public transport. Your student card then doubles as your ticket.

Bring to appointments

  • Passport or ID
  • Admission letter
  • Proof of health insurance
  • Passport photos
  • Copies of everything
