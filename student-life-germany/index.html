A typical order for your first weeks

Your university may do things in a slightly different order. Always check with its International Office.

1

Arrange health insurance

You usually need proof of it to enrol.

2

Register your address

At the local registration office, soon after moving in.

3

Open a bank account

For rent, tuition-related fees and a salary if you work.

4

Enrol and get your student card

It also works as your semester ticket at many universities.

A guide, not official advice

Rules about visas, insurance and work change, and they differ by country of origin and by city. Use this site to understand the basics, then confirm the details with official sources such as your university, the DAAD or Make it in Germany.
